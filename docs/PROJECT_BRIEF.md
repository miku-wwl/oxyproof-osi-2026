# OxyProof — Project Brief

**Repository:** `oxyproof-osi-2026`

**Title:** OxyProof: Evidence-Grounded Oxygen-Ion Conductor Discovery & Scientific Verification Agent

**Pitch:** A research agent that goes beyond recommending materials: it calculates ionic conductivity using published Arrhenius models, shows original experimental-source provenance, detects invalid physical assumptions, and abstains when the evidence is insufficient.

**Target event:** https://llmhackathon.github.io/ (October 21–22, 2026; deadline according to 2026 agenda: October 23, 13:00 NZDT; organizer updates may supersede).

**Primary source:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18947543; Jang et al., Scientific Data (2026), https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07100-x

**Expected demo:** Ask for high conductivity at 600 °C; inspect ranked evidence-backed candidates; open a candidate's source/provenance; inspect Arrhenius curves; toggle a demonstrably incorrect equation; see an audit warning and verifiable calculation trace.

**Scientific caution:** Candidate rankings are conditional model-derived screening, not new experiments or proven performance. CSV fields, thermal regimes, unit conventions and data-redistribution rights must be checked before coding or bundling a fixture.

**Prior winners to differentiate from:** https://llmhackathon.github.io/awards/ (2025 top projects in synthesis exploration and many other materials agents).
