# Contribution and claim boundaries

OxyProof contributes a reproducible decision workflow over published fitted transport parameters: temperature intervals, exact-source comparability, analytic root checks, regime-boundary review, fail-closed request coverage, independently audited tool execution and a browser research workbench. It does not claim that the underlying Arrhenius algebra, rank reversal, provenance or scientific agents are unprecedented.

Jang et al. collected and curated the dataset, including their own parameter replotting/reassessment. Those scientific contributions belong to the source authors. OxyProof neither discovered these parameters nor experimentally discovered a material. The row 1/2/3 example is a computed interpretation of their curated evidence.

The source group's common DOI/type/method/range is a **provisional** comparability policy. Atmosphere, preparation and density are missing columns, so even within-group screening needs original-paper review. Composition labels are original dataset strings. Parameters, transition temperatures and predicted crossing locations have no validated error bars here. Stability, humidity tolerance, lifetime, cycling, synthesis, cost and toxicity cannot be established.

The independent offline coverage grammar is intentionally narrow, including in REAL_LLM mode. It can reject benign unfamiliar phrasing; users can use supported wording or explicit controls. Model integration is a small typed planner/executor over three allowlisted tools, not a claim of open-ended scientific reasoning. Mock transport contracts do not verify live model quality. Provider failures never become offline runs without user selection.

The v2 benchmark is 49 hand-selected cases, partly synthetic and overlapping with unit tests, without statistical sampling or significance claims. Browser checks cover Chromium on this Windows machine; they do not prove Safari, public hosting, mobile hardware or publisher-link availability. Source retrieval and clean-start commands are Windows-tested; a fresh POSIX machine has not been executed.

Original software is MIT under the owner's Phase 2 license request; data remain CC BY 4.0 and article rights remain separate. No article figures, source full text, credentials or browser binaries are redistributed by the project.
