## Novelty comparison

The official 2025 data describes first-place SKY (Materials Design Group) as adapting inorganic synthesis recipes from similar materials, and fourth-place CrystaLenz as a physics-based XRD analysis pipeline with provenance. This is a product focus comparison, not a claim that evidence audit or materials agents are unprecedented.
